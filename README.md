*“There is no end to the making of many books, and much study wearies the body.”*

Ecclesiastes 12:12

Welcome to The Online Lutheran Library! We are dedicated to providing Martin Luther’s works and Lutheran resources in English both digitally and freely. Here you can find English translations of Luther's writings as well as links and bibliographies to other Lutheran resources accessible on the web. We suggest that you buy second-hand those materials that are only accessible in a physical format and sold profitably so that the sins of buying and selling Jesus are not supported. 
The inspiration for this site comes from the work of Conley Owens, Jon Here, and Andrew Case who advocate for the Biblical principle of supporting, not selling, ministry. If you're new to this teaching, please check out [The Dorean Principle](https://thedoreanprinciple.org) by Owens which sets out what Christ taught about money and ministry. [SellingJesus.org](http://SellingJesus.org) is also a great place to find articles on applying the Dorean Principle. Other freely given resources can be found at [freely.giving](https://freely.giving). Sadly, the vast majority of the resources listed here are not freely given nor dedicated to the public domain; however, it is my hope that access to these resources online will help to prevent any unnecessary participation in the sin of buying or selling Jesus. It is also my hope that this site will allow those who cannot afford profitable Lutheran resources to still have maximal access to those that they can legally have for free. Resources that are freely given (Matt. 10:8) are linked in green. 
Do you see a mistake? Have a resource to request or recommend? Or want to get involved? Please reach out! Just send an email to FreelyGivenLuther@gmail.com. I would be more than happy to hear from you.
Please note that I have tried my best to only provide access to resources that I assume are in obedience with copyright laws. It is not my intent to condone piracy or other illegal forms of sharing materials. If you think that your resource is illegally hosted at a link given here, please reach out at the email above. After a demonstration of its illegality, I will remove the link from this page. I do pray, however, that before doing so you will choose to explore the generosity of freely giving.

# How to Navigate the Online Lutheran Library

The Online Lutheran Library is designed to provide all of the accessible digital Lutheran materials on a certain subject to the visitor in one convenient place. The result of this system is that certain documents that are similar in origin are found in various places. For example, the documents from the Lutheran Church–Missouri Synod’s Commission on Theology and Church Relations are sorted separately into their respective sections on Sexuality, Marriage, Church and State, etc. instead of being collected together into a single block. When there is not a home for a Lutheran resource on a subject because its topic is too select, it will be categorized under its church or organization’s division, which is found after the subjects. Finally, historical sources are gathered at the end of the library and are sorted by topic or church. As a rule of thumb, the priorities of sorting a resource are:

1. The Book of Concord and related materials (e.g. *corpora doctrinae* and historical introductions)

2. Works of Luther or a Lutheran father (e.g. Luther, Melanchthon, Chemnitz, Walther, Krauth). 

3. Matter related to a wide-spread topic (e.g. the Bible, Sexuality, Evolution)

4. Lutheran History

5. Remaining material by originating body

The one exception to the system above is that periodicals are kept together for order’s sake. For example, a work by Walther in *Lehre und Wehre* will be found among those volumes rather than in his division the Lutheran father section.

If ever you cannot find something be sure to try ctrl+F (or cmd+F for a Mac) and search this library for the topic, work, or key word you wish to find. Don’t forget to utilize synonyms or less words in your search for more matches.
